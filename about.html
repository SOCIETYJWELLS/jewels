The House of Society

For decades, Society Jewellers has been synonymous with uncompromised artistry, royal heritage, and ethically sourced fine gold and diamond treasures.

Crafting Timeless Legacies Since 1985

Founded on the principles of royal Indian artistry and flawless gem selection, Society Jewellers began as a boutique studio catering to royalty and discerning collectors.

Every creation in our Vault passes through the hands of master artisans whose lineages span generations. From 22K BIS Hallmarked gold to certified EF-VVS solitaires, our jewelry is created to be passed down through time as precious family heirlooms.

We invite you to experience our flagship showroom lounge, where privacy, heritage, and personalized bespoke design consultations await you.
